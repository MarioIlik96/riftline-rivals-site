Riftline Rivals Support

Need help with Riftline Rivals? Send us the details and we will review your issue as soon as possible.

Email support Privacy Policy

What To Include

  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • The game version and build number if visible in Settings.
  • The chapter or screen where the issue happened.
  • Whether the issue happened after an ad, revive, upgrade, or restart.
  • Screenshots or a short video if they help explain the problem.

Purchases And Ads

Riftline Rivals uses platform billing for purchases when purchases are enabled. Rewarded ads are optional and should only grant rewards after the ad provider reports that the reward was earned.

If a reward, purchase, or restore does not work, email support@riftlinerivals.com with the approximate time, the screen, and what you expected to receive.
