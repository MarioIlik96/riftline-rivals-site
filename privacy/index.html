Riftline Rivals Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026

This policy applies to Riftline Rivals and must match the exact build, SDKs, and providers submitted to Google Play or the App Store.

Overview

Riftline Rivals is a mobile hook-duel game. The first release does not require an account and does not include chat or user-generated content.

  • The game does not require an account.
  • The game does not use precise location.
  • The game does not use contacts.
  • The game does not use health data.
  • The game does not use financial account data.
  • The game does not use social graph data.

Data Stored On Device

The local save can store chapter progress, replay grades, Rivets and Gleam balances, upgrade ranks, cosmetic ownership, equipped cosmetics, story state, rival state, daily challenge state, tutorial state, revive state, rewarded-ad cap state, audio settings, privacy settings, save backup, and recovery metadata.

This local save is used for app functionality and progression recovery. It is not an account profile and is not shared with other players.

Analytics

If analytics forwarding is enabled by the player, the game may send gameplay and balance events such as level start, level complete, level fail, fail reason, hook throws, hits, misses, upgrade purchases, perk choices, economy sources and sinks, daily challenge results, store views, rewarded-ad flow events, and IAP flow events.

Analytics events are used to improve balance, stability, economy pacing, and feature quality. Analytics forwarding is disabled until the player enables the analytics consent setting.

Crash Reporting

If crash reporting is enabled by the player and a production crash provider is configured, the game may send startup errors, Flutter errors, platform errors, and crash diagnostics. Crash reporting is used to identify and fix stability issues. Crash forwarding is disabled until the player enables the crash reporting consent setting.

Rewarded Ads

The launch monetization plan uses opt-in rewarded ads only. The game does not use forced interstitial ads in the first release.

Rewarded ad placements can include:

  • double chapter Rivets
  • daily reward multiplier
  • extra daily challenge attempt
  • failure revive
  • failure comeback Rivets
  • shop refresh
  • upgrade milestone assist

Rewarded ad requests use non-personalized ads until the player enables personalized ads consent. A rewarded grant is issued only after the ad provider reports that the reward was earned.

In-App Purchases

Platform billing may be enabled for remove ads, starter pack, Gleam pack, and direct cosmetic bundle purchases. Purchases must use Google Play Billing on Android and Apple in-app purchase systems on iOS.

The launch plan does not include paid random rewards, paid loot boxes, external payment links, or paid combat advantage.

Data Sharing

When production providers are enabled, data may be processed by the configured analytics, crash reporting, ad, and platform billing providers for the purposes described in this policy. Store and ad providers may also process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

The game does not sell personal data. The game does not provide public profiles, chat, or user-generated content sharing in the first release.

Player Choices

The Settings screen includes controls for:

  • analytics consent
  • crash reporting consent
  • personalized ads consent
  • opening the public Privacy Policy URL
  • resetting local save data

Turning off a consent setting stops future forwarding controlled by that setting. Local progression data remains on the device unless the player resets the save or removes the app.

Children

Riftline Rivals is not directed to children under 13. Store age ratings must be answered from the final submitted build and final store metadata.

Changes

This policy should be updated whenever the submitted build changes its ads, analytics, crash reporting, billing, data collection, or privacy behavior. The store App Privacy and Play Data Safety forms must be updated at the same time.
